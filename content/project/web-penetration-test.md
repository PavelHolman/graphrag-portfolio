Semester project of the Ethical Hacking course at CTU FIT in summer 2024, done with a classmate. The two tested a company's web application from a local copy of its source code, from the perspective of an unauthenticated or free user trying to reach paid content, and wrote a report with a threat model and findings scored by CVSS severity. Pavel's part included authentication, session management, input validation and cryptography testing. The client stays unnamed.
