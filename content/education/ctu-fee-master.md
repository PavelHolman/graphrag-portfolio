Master's studies in Artificial Intelligence within Open Informatics at CTU's Faculty of Electrical Engineering, September 2025 to June 2027 (expected). Coursework: advanced algorithms, theory of algorithms, combinatorial optimization, AI planning, statistical and symbolic machine learning, deep learning and deep reinforcement learning (the latter two taken at Charles University and credited here), game theory, and Android and multiplatform mobile development, with PyTorch in coursework. Master's thesis: Applying Transformers to Combinatorial Problems, in Prof. Zdeněk Hanzálek's group, the same group that supervised the bachelor's thesis.
